# Actual-count information diagnostic for the joint profile panel

The optional-state selector reduces homogeneous variance but worsens two
low-depth minority comparisons. Before adding a sparse or segmented contrast
model,this diagnostic asks whether those failures persist when the generating
profiles and state law are known. It concerns the current24-region profile
panel,not the earlier600-cell gene-NB recovery or noiseless-rate experiment.

Use all290 exposed cases with their exact observed count matrices. Oracle
profiles and Gaussian state distribution come from the known generator;
actual per-cell latent state draws are not supplied to posterior inference.
Compare an iidpi=.2 membership prior with the generator's exact24-of120 prior.
The fixed-size posterior uses generating-polynomial coefficients,independently
checked with positive polynomial convolution in extended precision and small
exhaustive integer examples. This cannot become a count-only deployment rule.

Evaluate the same expected RNA and state-zero log-odds targets with all old
baselines and original32/64 comparison rules. Use96/192/384-node quadrature,
independent core/PMF reconstructions and adaptive probes on first-identifier
heterogeneous cases. All input identities,known profiles,state loadings and
population sizes must match the generator. The recorded numerical allowances
are arithmetic/convergence diagnostics,not biological confidence intervals.

If knowing parameters removes failures,parameter estimation or regularization
may offer room to improve. Persistent minority failures need different reasoning:
a whole-risk Bayes action need not minimize minority-only risk. Neither outcome
proves a universal biological power limit. Observationally equivalent DNA and
transcription explanations remain,independent DNA/replication remain necessary,
and biological n=0. No new model fit,default,threshold or calibration claim.
