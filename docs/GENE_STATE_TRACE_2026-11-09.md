Six locked mechanism cases reproduce the frozen raw-unit research candidate's
public tables and posterior arrays exactly. Their latent arrays reveal a concrete
computational defect in one four-profile state case: all six tumour-profile pairs
are exactly equal, but the merge step compares none. Only one tumour component

The estimand here is a distinct fitted RNA profile, not DNA clone identity.

equivalent in the expression-only, nonspatial model. A biological distinction
could still exist in independently measured DNA or alleles; RNA cannot establish
it. Near-equal profiles and low hard membership alone do not justify collapse.
