The [preceding qualification](INTERVAL_INCUMBENT_2026-11-11.md) passes its
computational and original mechanism gates but leaves both actual low-depth
two-fold outputs atK1. Better full-fitting objectives have not fixed recovery.
This test asks whether119-cell training preserves predictive membership
information that60-cell training loses, using exactly the two failures and
their homogeneous/state-only controls. It is a bounded performance experiment,
not a new biological cohort or a replacement of the original package criteria.

## Implemented intervention and decisive outcome

scratch. The held-out cell is absent from initialization, nuisance estimation,
interval search, optimization and stopping. Each family gets a fresh module;
no120-cell parameters or cached full-data witnesses enter training. The
qualified interval safeguard and shared-incumbent optimizer are unchanged.
Save every initial/final fit, interval proposal/failure, safeguard comparison
and stability step. Cell0-only perturbation must leave all12 training fit
records identical in each case.

Original score,diagnostic and selection function ASTs are identical to the
previous qualified exact-LOO procedure. The reader's independent calculation
helpers are identical. All four RNA bundles lock before any truth construction.
The reader independently checks selected proposal native gradients and PMFs,
every safeguard/shared step, every initial/final common384 objective, held-out
full-PMF scores, high-order/adaptive integrals, exact rational sums and selected
profiles. Numerical failure stops the chain; it is not treated as non-detection.

The fixed primary mechanism criterion is both low-depth cases jointK2 and both
controls jointK1. A failed case fails the complete criterion. Full descriptive
fits stay fixed from the preceding preflight, isolating selection from refit.
Report whole/minority RNA and state-zero errors, simple baselines, membership,
numerical allowances and any regressions alongside the criterion. Original
combined/fold numerical uncertainty is retained. No favorable subset, smaller
diagnostic or changed support threshold can rescue a failed result.

The estimand is held-out RNA count prediction and conditional state-zero RNA
response under the existing model. Regional transcription remains a competing
genomic explanation. Biological n=0; LOO fits overlap. A positive predictive
score is not a5% false-split guarantee. Public component support30, original
production recovery2/5, state false-splits14/200,power195/200,selected coverage
54/1000 and all original32/64 gates remain. Independent DNA at the claimed
resolution and suitable biological replication remain necessary. No public
method, frozen artifact, consumed dataset role or scientific criterion changes.
