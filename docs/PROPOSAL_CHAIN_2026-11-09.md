## Scientific contract and decisive test

The estimand is where candidate group information is lost in these fixed inputs,
conditional on the fitted reference/nuisance model. Event proposals use the
executed NB mixture model; Leiden and confirmation remain unchanged. A competing
explanation is that informative proposals exist but ranking or test-half
agglomeration discards them, rather than the proposal half never separating the
classes. The post-fit truth contingency tables can distinguish those mechanisms
in this simulator. They cannot establish genomic meaning in biological material,
where regional cell state and reference mismatch remain alternatives.

The frozen [design](../dev/proposal_chain_20261109/DESIGN.md) records ten complete
workflows, biological n=0, all six original recovery criteria, exact public-output
parity, independent selection replay and SciPy likelihood calculations. Capture
opens no truth labels. The audit joins source row IDs to truth only after fitting,
reports represented/total tumour cells and retains every group contingency table.
Majority accuracy is a known-label descriptive summary, not prospective clone
discovery or membership calibration; many-part partitions can inflate it.

Successful event-window fits are observed before eligibility, ranking and Jaccard filtering.
Unresolved fit exceptions retain their original diagnostic records in `log.tsv`.
Actual bin coordinates determine chromosome identity: numbers in event source
strings enumerate reset blocks and are not assumed to be genomic chromosomes.
The trace also records returned candidates, initial/confirmation agglomeration,
candidate scores and merge assignments. Read-only wrappers preserve the executed
algorithm. Any output parity failure invalidates the observational comparison.
