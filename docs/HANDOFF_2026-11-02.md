## 9. Gotchas learned the hard way

- The scratchpad (`/scratch/...`) is node-local: compute jobs cannot see it. Put job scripts and outputs under
  `~/Haplodose/dev/` or `results/`.
- `sbatch --constraint=X` on the command line does not override `--prefer=v6` in the script; pin inside the
  script.
- Node generation changes results slightly (v4 vs v5/v6: ~0.004 ARI on Kinker v3, Andor SNU-601 AUROC 0.76 vs
  0.82). Run A/B arms on one pinned generation.
- `rm -rf $VAR/...` is blocked by a safety check; use `rsync --exclude` or literal paths, or ask the user.
- sacct `MaxRSS` needs `MaxRSS%30 --noconvert` and stripping. `/usr/bin/time` misses forked R workers; take the
  larger of the two.
- SCEVAN's `plotConsensusCNA` crashes with 10 or more subclones; `scevan_run.R` replaces it with a no-op.
- ENA HTTPS gives 403 pages and truncations; use FTP with size-checked resume.
- Kinker cell id is `<barcode>-<channel>-<pool>`; the ENA title is `Pool<channel>_<pool>`.
- No env has openpyxl or readxl; avoid Excel inputs or ask before adding a dependency.
